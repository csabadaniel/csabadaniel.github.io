Milan Trip Itinerary

Travelling Party

  • 2 adults
  • 2 children: boy aged 13, girl aged 10

Dates

  • Maximum date range (Brooklands College half term + surrounding weekends): 24 October 2026 to 1 November 2026

Recommended Trip Length & Day Plan

  • Recommended length: 5 days total (including arrival and departure days)
  • Why this works: enough time for Milan highlights plus one full Ferrari Museum day trip without rushing children

High-Level Day-by-Day (5 Days)

  1. Day 1 (Arrival in Milan): check-in, local walk, light sightseeing near hotel area, easy dinner
  2. Day 2 (Milan Core): Duomo (interior + rooftop), Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, Castello Sforzesco
  3. Day 3 (Ferrari Day): full-day train trip to Modena + transfer to Maranello for Ferrari Museum
  4. Day 4 (Milan Interests): science museum and/or San Siro, plus flexible family activity (pizza-making or gelato/chocolate workshop)
  5. Day 5 (Departure): light final activity and transfer to airport

If flight times are very early outbound or very late inbound, consider adding a 6th day for a more relaxed pace.

  • UK departure airport: London Stansted (STN)
  • UK return airport: London Stansted (STN)
  • Airline preference: Ryanair (to keep costs down)
  • Open-jaw across Milan airports allowed: Yes (arrive and depart from different Milan-area airports if useful)

Best-Fit Hotel Areas (Milan)

Target hotels with public-transport times of 45 minutes or less to both Duomo and Milano Centrale.

  • Sesto Marelli / Sesto San Giovanni (M1): best value-to-connection balance; direct metro to central Milan and practical for family budgets
  • Lambrate / Città Studi: strong overall balance of price, local environment, and reliable transport links
  • Turro / Gorla / Precotto (M1 corridor): practical budget-focused areas with straightforward metro access
  • Bande Nere / Gambara / De Angeli (west side): calmer residential feel with good metro links into the centre
  • Affori / Dergano / Maciachini: usually better value with solid public transport, especially around Maciachini as a transport node

For the Whole Family

  • Ferrari Museum (Maranello, near Modena) — simulators, iconic cars, and a motorsport history that appeals to all ages; easy full-day trip from Milan. Getting there: train from Milan Centrale to Modena (typically ~2 hours with one change), then SETA bus 780 to Maranello (~25 min); buses are infrequent — check timetable in advance.
  • Milan’s Duomo — interior & rooftop walk — the soaring Gothic interior is unmissable; the rooftop terraces are hands-on and give great views over the city
  • Pizza, chocolate and gelato workshops — widely available in Milan; hands-on, interactive and memorable for all ages

Especially for the 13-year-old Boy

  • Leonardo Museum of Science & Technology — interactive science exhibits, full-scale ship models, historical technology; one of the largest science museums in Europe
  • FC Internazionale / AC Milan stadium tour (San Siro) — stadium tour and museum; football-mad boys will love it. Getting there: Metro M5 (Lilla) to San Siro Stadio, or M1 (Rossa) to Lotto then ~20 min walk; not central but very easy by metro.

Especially for the 10-year-old Girl

  • Castello Sforzesco — a real castle with towers and a moat; castle exploration is reliably exciting at this age
  • Leonardo3 Museum — interactive models and digital reconstructions of Leonardo’s inventions; engaging for older children and easy to combine with Duomo/Galleria visits

Parents’ Must-Sees

  • The Last Supper (Cenacolo Vinciano) — Leonardo da Vinci’s mural; tickets must be booked months in advance, strictly timed entry; non-negotiable if passing through Milan
  • Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II — one of the world’s oldest and most beautiful shopping arcades; worth a stroll and an overpriced coffee

Seasonal Notes (Late October)

  • Halloween season means some attractions run themed events — check dates carefully
  • Autumn weather is usually good for museums, city walking, and day trips in Lombardy and Emilia-Romagna
  • Crowds are low compared to summer; many outdoor sites are pleasant to visit
